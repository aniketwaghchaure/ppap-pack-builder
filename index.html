PPAP Pack Builder

Runs entirely in your browser. Part numbers, drawings and process data never leave this device. Nothing is uploaded, logged or seen by anyone else.

Part & submission level

Fill this once. Everything downstream (which elements you must submit, the PFMEA and control-plan headers, the PSW draft) is driven from here. Example data loaded — replace with your part

Level 3 is the AIAG default unless your customer's authorised representative specifies otherwise. Many Tier-1s in Chakan and PCMC specify their own level and format in their Customer-Specific Requirements; the CSR always wins over this tool.

Process flow & characteristics

One block per operation. Each characteristic you add here becomes a line in the control plan and a seed row in the PFMEA, so you type it once. Mark special characteristics (CC / SC) — the control plan carries the symbol and the PFMEA pre-sets severity.

Process FMEA

Rate S, O, D on 1–10. Action Priority (AP) is computed from the AIAG-VDA table; RPN is shown for customers still on the AIAG 4th-edition format. Rows seeded from characteristics stay linked to them.

H act — action required M should act, justify if not L could act

Control plan

Generated from the characteristics in the process flow. Edit the evaluation method, sample size, frequency, control method and reaction plan here; the characteristic itself is edited in tab 2.

Columns follow the AIAG control-plan layout (part/process no., process name, machine, characteristics, special class, spec/tolerance, evaluation technique, sample size & frequency, control method, reaction plan).

PPAP checklist —

The 18 elements with what your chosen level asks for. Track status and owner per element; readiness counts only the elements you must have for this level.

Level codes cross-checked against the AIAG PPAP 4th-edition retention/submission table as reproduced in several Tier-1 supplier manuals. Your customer's CSR overrides it. Bulk-material requirements, if your customer demands them, are not covered here.

Export

One workbook with five sheets: Process Flow, PFMEA, Control Plan, PPAP Checklist and a PSW header draft. Open it in Excel, apply your customer's format, and submit.

What this tool does not do

It does not replace the AIAG manuals or your customer's CSR, does not do MSA, capability or dimensional studies, and does not know your process. Severity, occurrence and detection ratings are yours to defend in the audit.
